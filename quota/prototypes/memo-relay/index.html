QUOTA

Tell your friends. Quickly.

Same computer, two tabs:
Move: WASD / arrows. Pick up and drop: E or Space. Shout memo to bots (solo): F. Results and next steps: Enter (host). Vote on directives: 1, 2 or 3.
M music, N sound effects, C screen effect. Voice chat stays on Discord. Only one player sees each memo.

HOW TO PLAY

You and your friends are Ministry clerks. Sort the files off the belt before the shift timer ends. Reach the quota to pass. Miss it and the run is over. Talk on voice chat the whole time.

Controls

  • WASD or arrows to move. E or Space to grab a file and to drop it in a tray.
  • F shout a memo to the bots (solo only). 1 2 3 vote on directives. Enter moves on (host).
  • M music, N sound, C screen effect, R reduce motion, P pause (solo), L lock the room (host), Esc twice to leave. A game controller works too.

Scoring

Each correct file scores your team's multiplier. Every 4 correct files in a row raise it (up to x5). Any mistake breaks the streak.

The shifts

  • Shift 1: files go to the tray of their own colour. Learn the controls.
  • Shift 2, need to know: trays are numbered and shuffled. Each colour has a registrar, the one player who can see where it goes. Ask each other!
  • Shift 3, stamp desk: one at a time, stamping takes a moment. Some memos say a colour must be stamped.
  • Memos: every so often the Ministry orders a rule change. Only one player sees it. Everyone sees that one was issued. Tell the others before it takes effect.
  • Directives: after each pass, vote for one of three rule cards. They last for the whole run.
  • Shift 10: reach it and the Ministry has a surprise for you.
